FIGURE AND ANALYSIS SOURCE INVENTORY
====================================

This inventory links every retained figure or analysis to its generating code,
inputs, and supporting outputs. Published figures, validation analyses,
additional analyses, and archived material are listed separately.


PUBLISHED MAIN-TEXT FIGURES
---------------------------

Figure 1: Phoenix Metropolitan Area and constituent water-provider areas
  Output: figures/figure_1.png
  Code: scripts/figure_1.R; scripts/figure_1_provider_map.R
  Inputs: data/spatial/

Figure 3: frequency distribution of 2060 GSI across 24,300 scenarios
  Output: figures/figure_3.png
  Code: scripts/figure_3.R; scripts/common_data.R
  Input: SINYRGWR in 2060 from data/merged_ob_data.csv.gz

Figure 4: population distribution at each exact realized GSI
  Output: figures/figure_4.png
  Code: scripts/figure_4.R; scripts/common_data.R
  Inputs: 2060 SINYRGWR and the POPUSED regional-population lookup

Figure 5: factor-level median population frontiers and IQR ribbons
  Output: figures/figure_5.png
  Code: scripts/figure_5.R; scripts/common_data.R
  Supporting tables: tables/frontier_ribbon_summary/
  Validation: internal_validation/frontier_bounds/
  Definition: for each climate-policy combination and GSI threshold 79-98,
  the frontier is the highest modeled 2060 population among the tested
  population levels that meets GSI >= the threshold.

Figure 6: conditional-feasibility heatmaps for all six non-population factors
  Output: figures/figure_6.png
  Code: scripts/figure_6.R; scripts/common_data.R
  Definition: each panel fixes one factor level; each cell reports the percent
  of combinations of the remaining factors meeting the population and minimum
  GSI requirement.

Figure 7: Lake Mead, Lake Powell, and Salt-Verde storage trajectories
  Output: figures/figure_7.png
  Code: scripts/figure_7.R; scripts/common_data.R
  Inputs: MEDSTORE, POWSTORE, and SVRSTORE from 2000-2060

SUPPORTING VALIDATION
---------------------

Figure 5 boundary-identifiability analysis
  Location: internal_validation/frontier_bounds/
  Purpose: evaluates censoring across all GSI thresholds and supports selection
  of the 79-98 interval used in Figure 5.

Matched-frontier analysis
  Location: internal_validation/matched_frontier/
  Purpose: compares factor levels within identical backgrounds of all other
  non-population factors. The directory contains methods, a figure caption,
  scripts, figures, and supporting tables.


ADDITIONAL ANALYSIS
-------------------

Overall unconditioned feasibility heatmap
  Location: additional_analysis/overall_feasibility/
  Purpose: shows the percentage of all 324 climate-policy combinations meeting
  each population and minimum-GSI requirement without fixing a focal factor.
