SUPPLEMENTARY FIGURE 1
----------------------
Subject: Relationship between the WaterSim5 population-growth input and final
regional population in 2060.

Final figure:
figures/supplementary_figure_1.png

Generating scripts:
scripts/supplementary_figure_1.R
scripts/common_data.R

Required data:
data/population_growth_data.csv.gz

Fields and calculation:
POPUSED is filtered to 2060 and summed across all 33 providers for each
population-growth scenario. The figure includes all 75 factorial WEBPOPGR
levels from 2 through 150 in increments of 2. The additional WEBPOPGR 0
scenario remains in the packaged data as a baseline but is not plotted.
