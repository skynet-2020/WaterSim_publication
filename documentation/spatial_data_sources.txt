SPATIAL DATA SOURCES AND PROCESSING
===================================

Purpose
-------
The spatial files in this repository support Figure 1. They are third-party
government geographic data or derived products. They are not simulation inputs
and are not covered by the repository's CC BY 4.0 license except for original
selection, organization, documentation, and processing contributed by Joshua
Gilman.


U.S. STATE BOUNDARIES
---------------------
Packaged file:
data/spatial/us_states_2024_cartographic.rds

Source:
U.S. Census Bureau, 2024 Cartographic Boundary Files, states.
https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.html

Processing:
The state polygons were obtained through the R spatial-data workflow and saved
as an sf RDS object. Figure 1 excludes Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico when
constructing the contiguous-U.S. locator map.

Reuse notice:
Works of the U.S. Government are not eligible for U.S. copyright protection.
The Census Bureau requests source attribution and states that these statistical
boundaries are not legal land descriptions. Census Bureau technical guidance:
https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/technical-documentation/complete-technical-documentation/tiger-geo-line.html


ACTIVE MANAGEMENT AREA AND IRRIGATION NON-EXPANSION AREA BOUNDARIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Packaged files:
data/spatial/phoenix_ama/AMA_and_INA.cpg
data/spatial/phoenix_ama/AMA_and_INA.dbf
data/spatial/phoenix_ama/AMA_and_INA.prj
data/spatial/phoenix_ama/AMA_and_INA.shp
data/spatial/phoenix_ama/AMA_and_INA.shx
data/spatial/phoenix_ama/AMA_and_INA.xml

Source:
Arizona Department of Water Resources (ADWR), AMA and INA boundary layer,
2024 edition. The layer identifies Arizona Active Management Areas and
Irrigation Non-Expansion Areas.
https://azwatermaps.azwater.gov/arcgis/rest/services/General/AMA_and_INA_2024/FeatureServer/0

Processing:
The complete source layer is preserved in the package. Figure 1 selects the
Phoenix AMA feature and transforms it for plotting.

Reuse and accuracy notice:
ADWR identifies itself as the copyright holder and supplies the data as-is,
without warranties concerning accuracy, completeness, quality, or legal
sufficiency. The underlying layer remains subject to ADWR's terms; this
repository does not relicense it. ADWR data disclaimer:
https://www.azwater.gov/data-disclaimer


WATER-PROVIDER BOUNDARIES
-------------------------
Packaged file:
data/spatial/ICB_provider_areas_transformed.rds

Sources:
1. Arizona Department of Water Resources Community Water System service-area
   boundaries, updated March 24, 2022.
   https://www.azwater.gov/cws/community-water-systems-data
2. Arizona incorporated-city boundaries maintained by the Arizona State Land
   Department through the Arizona Land Resource Information System, with input
   from the Arizona Department of Revenue. The local source metadata records an
   October 2021 publication date.
   https://azgeo.az.gov/arcgis/rest/services/asld/Cities/FeatureServer/0

Processing by Joshua Gilman:
- Selected large Community Water Systems in the Phoenix AMA that correspond to
  study providers.
- Used incorporated-city boundaries for providers associated with a city and
  Community Water System boundaries for the remaining mapped providers.
- Combined multiple Buckeye service-area records during intermediate
  processing.
- Standardized provider names and two-letter provider codes.
- Removed unused source attributes, combined the selected polygons, transformed
  the coordinate reference system, and saved the result as an sf RDS object.

Coverage limitation:
The derived map contains 29 provider areas. Four population-output providers
(Adaman Mutual, Berneil, Rigby, and West End) lacked matched spatial boundaries
and are therefore not shown in the provider-area map. This does not affect the
regional population calculation, which uses all 33 providers.

Reuse and accuracy notice:
The underlying Arizona geometries remain subject to their source-agency terms
and disclaimers. ADWR states that Community Water System boundaries may be
based on primary or secondary information and that spatial accuracy cannot be
verified. Arizona incorporated-city metadata states that the boundaries are not
legal records and disclaims warranties concerning their accuracy and fitness
for a particular purpose. This repository does not relicense the underlying
agency geometries.
